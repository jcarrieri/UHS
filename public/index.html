Welcome to Universal Healthcare Social

UHS is a free community for serious, respectful discussions about universal healthcare, how it works, and how to make healthcare accessible to everyone.

Members can follow people they choose, participate in healthcare-related conversations, and explore a broad range of topics, including public policy, economics and healthcare systems.

We believe universal healthcare is coming soon. Here you can exchange ideas, discuss evidence, and work toward better healthcare for all.

Always free. No ads. No algorithmic posts from people you do not follow.
